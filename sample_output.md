**Executive Brief: Saudi Arabia Vision 2030**

### Executive Summary
Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 establishes an overarching strategic blueprint anchored on three pillars: the Kingdom's status as the heart of the Arab and Islamic worlds, its determination to become a global investment powerhouse, and its role as an epicenter connecting Asia, Europe, and Africa. Structured across three themes—a vibrant society, a thriving economy, and an ambitious nation—the roadmap drives aggressive economic diversification to ensure the Kingdom is never again at the mercy of commodity price volatility. Key commercial catalysts include transforming Saudi Aramco into a global industrial conglomerate, expanding the Public Investment Fund (PIF) into the world's largest sovereign wealth fund with over SAR 7 trillion in assets, and unlocking new industrial sectors.

---

### Actionable Insights for Senior Leadership

#### 1. Develop Localized Renewable Energy and Industrial Value Chains
* **What to do:** Direct investment capital and operational planning toward domestic manufacturing and R&D for renewable equipment to capture a portion of the Kingdom's target to generate 9.5 gigawatts of renewable energy.
* **Why:** The Kingdom plans to liberalize fuel markets to ensure competitiveness, localize inputs like silica and petrochemicals, and partner with the private sector through the King Salman Renewable Energy Initiative.
* **Supporting Phrase:** *"To build up the sector, we have set ourselves an initial target of generating 9.5 gigawatts of renewable energy. We will also seek to localize a significant portion of the renewable energy value chain in the Saudi economy, including research and development, and manufacturing, among other stages."*

#### 2. Expand Domestic Gas and Upstream/Downstream Supply Chain Capabilities
* **What to do:** Align contracting, engineering, and service capabilities to secure market share in national gas infrastructure projects and domestic manufacturing, supporting the mandate to raise oil and gas localization to 75%.
* **Why:** National policy mandates reducing import reliance, creating dedicated energy hubs, and doubling domestic gas production to build adjacent industrial sectors.
* **Supporting Phrase:** *"To increase the localization of oil and gas sectors from 40% to 75%"* and *"As well as creating a new city dedicated to energy, we will double our gas production, and construct a national gas distribution network."*

#### 3. Partner with the Public Investment Fund (PIF) on Strategic Capital Deployment
* **What to do:** Structure co-investment vehicles and project development pipelines to participate in capital-intensive sectors alongside the PIF and prepare bids for government service privatizations.
* **Why:** The state is mobilizing the PIF as a non-competing partner to unlock high-barrier sectors, while transferring government-provided services to the private sector to raise private GDP contribution to 65%.
* **Supporting Phrase:** *"The Public Investment Fund will not compete with the private sector, but instead help unlock strategic sectors requiring intensive capital inputs. This will contribute towards developing entirely new economic sectors and establishing durable national corporations."*
